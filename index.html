Built with local media processing in mind.

Blurly is designed to blur and redact photos and videos on your device. Its core editing tools do not require uploading your selected media to Blurly-operated servers. Some optional or supporting features may use external services as described below.

01Introduction

Welcome to Blurly – Blur Face, Photo & Video (“Blurly,” “we,” “our,” or “the app”). Blurly provides editing tools to blur faces, obscure objects and sensitive information, and export edited media. This policy explains what information the app accesses or processes, why it is needed, and what choices you have.

02How we handle your media

Blurly's core photo and video editing functionality is designed to run on your device. Depending on the features you use, it can perform face detection and tracking, manual blur and redaction, preview generation, and photo or video export.

  • Selected photos and videos are accessed for the edits you request.
  • Core editing does not require uploading the selected media to Blurly-operated servers.
  • Projects and edits may be stored locally on your device.
  • Exported files are saved to supported device locations or shared with another app at your direction.

When you share an exported file through Android's sharing features, the destination app's privacy practices apply to that file.

03Information we process

Media and local project information

Blurly may store editing projects, selected media references, blur regions, export settings, app preferences, and feature entitlement status locally to make editing and project management work.

Technical and diagnostic information

Depending on which app services are enabled, third-party service providers may collect or process device model, operating system, app version, installation identifiers, crash diagnostics, app usage or performance signals, and notification tokens. The exact categories depend on the SDKs and features included in the installed app version.

Support communications

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, description of the issue, device/app details, and any attachments. Please avoid sending sensitive content unless it is needed for troubleshooting.

04Device permissions

Depending on your Android version and the features you choose, Blurly may request or use:

  • Photos, video, or storage access: to select, edit, save, and export media, including through Android's system photo picker where supported.
  • Notifications: to deliver notifications if notification features are enabled and you permit them.
  • Internet access: for enabled online services such as diagnostics, notifications, purchase verification, and other app services requiring connectivity.

You can manage available permission controls in Android settings. Some functions may be limited if necessary access is denied.

05Third-party services

Google Firebase

Blurly may use Google Firebase services, depending on the build and features enabled, for crash diagnostics, analytics, cloud messaging, performance monitoring, or application configuration. Enabled Firebase services may process technical information and identifiers to provide their respective functions. For details, see Firebase Privacy & Security and Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing

If premium purchases are offered, billing is handled by Google Play. The app may process purchase status, product identifiers, purchase tokens, and entitlement information to activate or restore premium access. We do not directly process full payment-card details.

The specific third-party SDKs used in the released app, and their disclosures, should match the app's Google Play Data safety form.

06Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Selected media is not sent to advertising or analytics services as part of core local editing. Technical, diagnostic, purchase, or support data may be processed by relevant service providers as needed for the enabled functionality described in this policy, or disclosed when legally required.

If you choose to share a photo or video using another app, that app is responsible for its own processing of what you send.

07Storage, retention & deletion

Blurly may keep project data and preferences locally until you delete them or remove the app's local data. You can delete supported projects using Blurly's project-management controls. Uninstalling Blurly might not remove exported files stored outside the app's private storage.

Service providers may retain technical data under their own retention policies and configurations. Support conversations may be retained as reasonably needed to resolve requests, comply with law, or maintain support records.

To request access to or deletion of personal information we control, contact connect.blurly@gmail.com. Some data managed by Google may need to be handled through Google.

08Security

Processing media locally reduces the need to transfer private photos or videos to a remote server. We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by Blurly and rely on the security practices of service providers for their systems. No transmission or storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure.

09Children's privacy

Blurly is a general-purpose media editing application intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe information about a child was collected in violation of applicable law, contact us so we can review and address the issue. Applicable local age-of-consent and child privacy laws may impose additional requirements.

10Free and premium features

Blurly may offer free features with limits, including export length, output resolution, tracked-face limits, or watermarks, along with premium upgrades. If offered, purchases are processed through Google Play and relevant purchase records may be used to verify and restore feature access.

11Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we control. You can manage Android permissions, delete projects, remove local app data, change notification settings, and manage Google Play purchases through the available controls. Email us for privacy-related requests.

12International data processing

Third-party providers used for enabled online functionality may process technical or operational information in countries outside your own, subject to their applicable safeguards and policies. Blurly's core editing is designed to process media locally on your device.

13Updates to this policy

We may update this policy as features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect revisions. Please check this page periodically.

14Contact us

Questions about privacy, support, or data requests? Get in touch with the Blurly team.
