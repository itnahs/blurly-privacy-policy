# Privacy Policy **Blurly – Blur Face, Photo & Video** **Effective Date:** November 3, 2026 **Last Updated:** November 3, 2026 ## 1. Introduction Welcome to Blurly. Blurly is a privacy-focused photo and video editing application designed to help users blur faces, hide sensitive information, redact objects, and protect personal content. We value your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is accessed, processed, stored, and shared when you use our application. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices, including how we handle photos and videos, app diagnostics, permissions, purchases, and support requests. By using Blurly, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. ## 2. Photo and Video Processing Blurly is designed to perform photo and video editing directly on your device. This includes: - Automatic face detection and tracking - Manual face and object blurring - Photo and video redaction - Blur effects and adjustments - Video preview generation - Photo and video exporting - Local project management **Your selected photos and videos are processed locally on your device.** Blurly does not require you to upload your photos or videos to our servers to use its core editing features. Your original media remains under your control. Exported files are saved to locations selected or supported by your device. ## 3. Information We May Process ### Photos and Videos When you select a photo or video, Blurly accesses the selected media to provide editing functionality. This media is processed on your device and is not transmitted to our servers as part of the core editing process. ### Local App Data Blurly may store information locally, including: - Saved editing projects - Blur regions and settings - Media references - Editor preferences - Export preferences - Premium entitlement status - Other settings required for app functionality This information is used to maintain your editing experience and preserve app functionality. ### Technical and Diagnostic Information Depending on the services enabled in your version of Blurly, limited technical information may be collected automatically. This may include: - Device model and operating system version - App version - App installation identifiers - Crash reports and diagnostic information - App performance or usage information - Notification delivery tokens This information may be processed through Google Firebase services to maintain reliability, diagnose problems, deliver notifications, and improve the application. ### Support Information If you contact our support team, we may receive information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address, device information, app version, description of the problem, and attachments. We use this information only to respond to your request, troubleshoot issues, and provide support. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in support messages unless necessary. ## 4. Permissions Blurly may request access to certain device features depending on your actions and Android version. ### Photos, Videos, and Storage Used to select, edit, save, and export photos and videos. Access may be provided through Android's system media picker or applicable storage permissions. ### Notifications Used to send app-related notifications, such as important updates or other notifications you have enabled. Notification permission may be requested on supported Android versions. ### Internet Access Used for supported online services, such as Firebase functionality, billing-related operations, and app services that require connectivity. The app's core photo and video editing features are designed to work locally. You can manage supported permissions through your device's Android settings. ## 5.
